About me

A quick look at how I work and what I've been building.

I'm a developer who enjoys the whole path from a rough idea to a shipped product — sketching the layout, wiring up the logic, and polishing the last 10% that makes something feel finished.

Most of my work sits at the intersection of design and engineering: building interfaces that are fast, accessible, and pleasant to use, backed by code that's easy for the next person to read.

Outside of client and personal projects, I spend time exploring new front-end tooling and contributing small fixes to open-source repos I rely on.
